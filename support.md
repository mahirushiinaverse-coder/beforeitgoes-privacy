# Before It Goes — Support

Before It Goes is one simple list for your household: things you have already paid for, with their use-by dates, and things due soon, like rent, bills and subscriptions.
It helps you use things and pay things before they cost you again — everything stays on your phone, with no account and no adverts.

## Common questions

### Where does my data live?

Only on your phone. Everything you add — your items, their costs and dates, your spending and your settings — is stored in the app's private storage on your own device. The app works fully offline for your list, has no server and sends none of your data to us or to any other company. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a barcode number you choose to scan, which the app sends to public product databases to fetch that product's details.

### What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Before It Goes removes all of its stored information with it, and deleted information is gone for good — there is no copy anywhere, and nobody can bring it back. You can also delete everything yourself at any time inside the app, in Settings, under "Delete all my data". The app will always warn you clearly and ask you to confirm before anything is deleted.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no Before It Goes account to create and no sign-in. You open the app and use your list straight away.
