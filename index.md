# Before It Goes — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 9 November 2026**
**Last updated: 9 November 2026**

This policy is the canonical, publish-ready privacy policy for the Before It Goes app. It is written to be published at a public web address (Apple requires a privacy policy URL for every app, including apps that collect nothing) and to match the short version shown inside the app. It describes the app exactly as it works today.


---

## 1. The short version

Before It Goes keeps everything on your phone. Only on your phone.

We have no server and no account system. We do not collect, receive, track, sell or share any information about you or what you put in the app — there is nowhere for it to go, and nobody at Before It Goes can see it. There are no adverts, no trackers and no analytics tools in this app. One honest exception, explained in §3 and §12: if you scan a product barcode, the app looks that number up in public product databases so it can fill in the product's details for you. The barcode number is all that is sent — never your name, your list, or anything about you.

## 3. What the app stores, and where

Everything you add in Before It Goes — the items you have paid for, their costs, use-by dates, bills and due dates, your daily spending entries, your settings and preferences — is stored only in the app's private storage **on your own device**.

- Nothing you enter is sent to us or to any other company.
- The app works fully offline for everything you add by typing, speaking or photographing a label — none of that ever uses the internet.
- The one time the app goes online is when **you** scan a product barcode: it sends just the barcode number — the number printed on the packet, which identifies the product, not you — to the public product databases named in §12, to fetch that product's details, and it shows the product's picture from those databases. Your items, amounts and dates are never part of that request.
- There is no Before It Goes account to create, and no sign-in.

## 4. What we collect

**Nothing.** To be specific, we do not collect:

- personal details (we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or address);
- the items, amounts, dates or notes you enter;
- usage statistics, behaviour or analytics of any kind;
- device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or fingerprints;
- your location;
- crash reports or diagnostics sent to us;
- anything for tracking you across apps or websites — the app contains no tracking technology and no third-party advertising or analytics software of any kind.

Apple's App Privacy label for this app reads **"Data Not Collected"**, and that is the whole truth of how the app is built.

## 5. Permissions the app may ask for, and what they are used for

The app asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no, and the rest of the app still works. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

- **Microphone (voice add).** Used only when you tap the voice button. Your speech is turned into text **on your phone itself**. Your voice is never recorded for keeping, never sent to us, and never sent to any server — and if your phone cannot turn speech into text on the device, voice add simply will not start, rather than sending your voice elsewhere.
- **Camera (scan).** Used only when you tap scan, to read a packet, a date, a price or a bill, on the device. If you scan a product barcode, the app looks that number up as described in §3 so it can name the product for you. A photo is kept only if you choose to save one (for example, a receipt photo).
- **Photos.** Used only when you pick a photo yourself: it is read on the device — to fill in a packet's details from its label, or to keep as an item's picture. Photos are never sent anywhere.
- **Notifications.** Before It Goes schedules reminders **on your phone** — for example, that something is about to pass its use-by date or a bill is due. These are local reminders created by the app on your device; no message is sent from any server.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** If you choose to set up a shortcut, it runs on your device under your own settings. We receive nothing from it.

## 6. Household sharing — only if you choose it

Household sharing is an **optional** feature. It is off unless you turn it on.

When you choose to use Household sharing, the list you choose to share is stored in **your own iCloud account** (using Apple's iCloud service) so the people you invite can see it on their devices. That information passes between your devices and the people you invite, through your iCloud account — it never comes to us, we cannot see it, and we have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

You stay in control: you choose what to share and who to invite, and you can stop sharing or leave a household at any time. If you never turn sharing on, nothing ever leaves your phone.

## 7. How long your information is kept

For exactly as long as it stays on your phone. Because we never receive any of it, there is nothing kept by us, anywhere, for any length of time.

## 8. Deleting your information — please read this carefully

You are in full control of deletion, at any time, without asking anyone:

- **Delete everything in the app:** Settings → "Delete all my data" removes everything you added.
- **Delete the app:** removing Before It Goes from your phone removes all of its stored information with it.
- **Household sharing:** if you shared a list through your own iCloud, removing it from the shared place in the app, or leaving the household, removes it from sharing; copies already on other household members' own devices are theirs to delete.

**The honest warning:** because everything lives only on your phone, deleted information is gone for good. There is no copy anywhere — not with us, not in any cloud account of ours — and neither we nor anyone else can bring it back. The app will always warn you clearly and ask you to confirm before anything is deleted.

## 9. Your rights

Privacy laws around the world — including the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA in California — give people rights over their personal information, such as the rights to see it, correct it, move it, and have it deleted.

Before It Goes is built so you exercise those rights yourself, directly, because your information never leaves your device:

- **See it:** everything you entered is visible in the app.
- **Correct it:** edit any entry at any time.
- **Delete it:** use "Delete all my data" in Settings, or delete the app (see §8).
- **Control it:** permissions can be granted or withdrawn in your phone's Settings; Household sharing is your choice alone.

As we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or erase on our side — and nothing we could hand to anyone else. If you believe we hold any personal information about you (for example, an email you sent us), contact us using §2 and we will deal with your request as the law where you live requires.

## 10. Children

Before It Goes is a household budgeting tool made for adults. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone — of any age. (As §4 explains, it collects nothing at all.)

## 11. Security

Your information is protected by the security of your own device — the app's private storage, your device passcode and your device's built-in encryption. Your information itself is never transmitted at all, so there is no transmission of it for anyone to intercept and no company database of it that could be breached. The only thing that ever leaves the phone is a barcode number you choose to scan (§3), which identifies a product, not you.

## 12. Other companies

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. Two kinds of outside services exist. The first is Apple's — the App Store, your device's operating system, and (only if you turn it on) your own iCloud account for Household sharing; Apple's handling of those services is described in Apple's privacy policy. The second is used only when you scan a product barcode (§3): the public product databases the app asks for that product's details — Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Beauty Facts (run by the Open Food Facts non-profit) and UPCitemdb. Those databases receive the barcode number and the ordinary technical details any web request carries (such as an IP address), under their own privacy policies. They receive nothing about you or your list from the app, and the app's developer receives nothing from them about you. If a paid "Pro" upgrade is offered in the future, payment will be handled entirely by Apple; we will never see or store your payment details.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new words will be published at the same web address and shown in the app before they apply, with a new "Last updated" date at the top. If a future version of the app ever changed how information is handled, the updated policy would say so plainly first — and the app's App Store privacy label would be updated to match.

## 14. Where this policy applies

This policy is published for users everywhere in the world. Nothing in it limits any right you have under the privacy law of the country or state where you live.
